# Interpretation and limitations

Cross-sectional association; no temporal or causal inference. Complete-case selection may bias estimates. MEC weights address the sample design, not all item missingness. Age is top-coded at 80; linear age adjustment may leave residual confounding. Treatment, lifestyle and other unmeasured factors are not adjusted. Sparse BMI bands have wider intervals. Results describe the 2017-2018 cycle, not current health status. The analysis is an independent public-data portfolio project, unaffiliated with Kaiser Permanente; no clinical recommendations.

The DIQ010=2 subgroup is based on reported diagnosis, not absence of diabetes. Pregnant adults are not excluded in this fixed minimal scope. The race-adjusted and quadratic specifications are sensitivity analyses, not chosen as a new primary model.
